# Chinese-To-English Scientific Rewriting

## Workflow

1. Extract the scientific claim from the Chinese sentence.
2. Identify evidence and boundary.
3. Reorder into English scientific logic: context, claim, evidence, implication, limitation.
4. Choose a PNAS-appropriate level of breadth.
5. Polish sentence rhythm only after logic is stable.

## Common Chinese Draft Patterns

- Background first, claim late: move the claim earlier when drafting Results.
- Many linked clauses: split into shorter English sentences.
- Broad significance language: convert to a precise consequence or testable implication.
- Implicit caveats: make model assumptions and parameter ranges explicit.
- Repeated nouns: use consistent terms, but avoid dense noun stacks.

## Output

For difficult passages, provide:

- polished English
- literal meaning preservation note
- claim/evidence/boundary check
- one alternate version if tone is uncertain
